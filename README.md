# 🔐 SynCryptAI — Private AI Cloud OS

> Your data. Your machine. Your AI. Nobody else's business.

## What is SynCryptAI?

SynCryptAI is a complete private AI operating system running on Mac Mini M4.
It replaces Google Drive, iCloud, and cloud AI with a fully local, encrypted,
self-hosted alternative — controlled entirely from Telegram.

No cloud. No surveillance. No compromise.

## Who is it for?

- Lawyers, doctors, executives who need data privacy
- Privacy advocates escaping Big Tech
- Self-hosters who want local AI without API costs
- Anyone who believes their data should belong to them alone

## What it does

- 🤖 Local AI — Run Qwen, Gemma, DeepSeek, Whisper on your own hardware
- ☁️ Private Cloud — Nextcloud replaces Google Drive and iCloud
- 📱 Telegram Control — Manage everything from your phone
- 🔒 Biometric Security — AES-256-GCM encryption, PIN protection, biometric auth
- 🎙️ Voice Commands — Whisper transcribes locally, hands-free control
- 📧 Email Integration — Gmail IMAP processed locally, never touching Google servers
- 📊 Market Intelligence — Private AI analyst for signals and portfolio tracking
- 🖼️ Photo Privacy — Photoprism replaces Google Photos, fully local

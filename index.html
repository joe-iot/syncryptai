// What It Does

Everything you need.
Nothing they can see.

SynCryptAI replaces cloud services with a private, encrypted, AI-powered system that runs entirely on your hardware.

Local AI Hub
Run Claude, Gemini, Qwen, Gemma, DeepSeek locally or privately. Your conversations never leave your machine.
Private Cloud
Nextcloud replaces Google Drive, Photos, and iCloud. Your files, contacts, calendar - encrypted on your server.
Telegram Control
Control everything from your phone. Tasks, files, AI, finances - one bot does it all.
Military-Grade Security
AES-256-GCM encryption, PBKDF2 key derivation, biometric authentication, PIN-protected menus.
Voice Commands
Speak naturally. Whisper transcribes locally. Add items, set reminders, ask questions - hands-free.
Market Intelligence
Monitor channels, receive signals, analyze trades. Your private AI analyst - fully local.
Document Hub
OCR scanning, invoice extraction, auto-categorization. Process documents without any cloud.
Zero Cloud Dependency
Tailscale VPN gives secure remote access - without any public exposure.
Email Integration
Gmail IMAP connected locally. AI reads and categorizes emails - without touching Google's servers.
WhatsApp Backup
Automatic daily backup of all WhatsApp chats and media to your private Nextcloud. No Google. No iCloud. Fully encrypted and yours forever.
